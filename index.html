Welcome to the Study

Thank you for your interest in participating. Please read the following information before deciding whether you would like to take part.

About the study

This study examines how people perceive and evaluate online video content. You will be asked to watch one short video and answer several questions about your impressions of it.

What will you do?

After providing your consent, you will be randomly assigned to watch one of two videos. After watching the video, you will answer several questions about how you perceived the content.

Duration

The study should take approximately 3–5 minutes to complete.
