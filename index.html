Video Perception Study

Thank you for your interest in participating. In this short study, you will be asked to watch one short video and answer a few questions about your perception of the video. The study should take approximately 3–5 minutes to complete.

About the study

The purpose of this study is to understand how people perceive and evaluate online video content. You will be shown one video and asked to provide your impressions of it afterward.

Your participation

Participation is voluntary. You may choose not to participate or stop participating at any time. There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your personal impressions of the video you are shown.

Content notice

The video may contain discussion or imagery relating to immigration, social issues, or other potentially sensitive topics. Please consider this before deciding whether to participate.
